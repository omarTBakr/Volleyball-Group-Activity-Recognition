Shaded bands: "per player" layers run on every player separately with shared weights; "whole scene" layers run once per clip. B6's skip: the pooled per-frame features are projected to the LSTM width and concatenated with the LSTM's 9 outputs along time (18 steps); a Conv1d with a 2T kernel collapses them to one clip vector. B7's first skip concatenates each player's projected features with LSTM1's output along the feature axis; its second is B6's time-axis skip after LSTM2. B7 pools max and mean per frame.
